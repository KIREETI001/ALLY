What ALLY does

Six features that turn caregiving from a panic moment into a plan.

Each feature exists because a caregiver told us they couldn't handle the moment without it. Each one is calibrated for Singapore — its hospitals, its schemes, its languages, its households.

📄

Discharge summary parser

Drop in your hospital discharge document. ALLY extracts medications, wound care, warning signs, follow-ups, and turns them into a structured care plan in under 30 seconds — with a confirmation step so you stay in control.

💰

Subsidy navigator

Five questions. ALLY tells you which government schemes you qualify for, with current 2026 amounts — including the new S$600/mo Home Caregiving Grant. Most users find S$2,000–8,000/year they didn't know existed.

👥

Care team coordination

Invite your spouse, siblings, and helper. Everyone sees the same task list. Ownership is explicit. The auditable log ends weekly arguments at the family WhatsApp meeting.

🌏

Five languages, fully

English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil, and Filipino — not just translated buttons but translated medication explanations, JIT videos, and subsidy guides. The helper, the auntie, the parent: included.

🌙

Burnout monitoring

ALLY watches the signs you don't notice yourself — completion times slipping, mood logs trending down, chat sentiment shifting. Gentle nudges, never alarms. Respite care surfaced when you need it.

💬

AI co-pilot

A chat companion grounded in Singapore healthcare, schemes, and clinical guidelines. Not a doctor, not a diagnoser — a knowledgeable friend who remembers everything you told it about your loved one.

Trust & safety

Built for the highest-stakes data on your phone.

Your loved one's health information sits in the most regulated category of personal data in Singapore. ALLY is engineered for that responsibility from the first line of code.

🔒

Singapore data residency

All personal data stored in the AWS / Supabase Singapore region. No cross-border transfers without explicit consent and risk assessment.

📜

PDPA compliant

Published Data Protection Notice, designated Data Protection Officer, breach notification process. Aligned to MOH/PDPC Healthcare Sector Advisory Guidelines (2023 revision).

⚕️

Care coordination, not a medical device

ALLY positions outside HSA's SaMD perimeter under the 2025 CDSS exemption — surfacing guideline-grounded information, never diagnostic or prescriptive output.

🛡️

Human-in-the-loop AI

Every parsed care plan is confirmed by a human before activation. Emergency keywords route to 995. AI output carries a clear disclaimer in every response.

🔐

Encrypted end-to-end

TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest. Role-based access. Quarterly access audits. Cyber Essentials certification target before any public-sector partnership.

👤

Your data, your control

Export everything. Delete everything. Revoke any team member's access. We never sell data. We never train AI models on your loved one's health information.

Questions

Frequently asked.

If your question isn't here, write to us at hello@ally.sg — we read every message.

Is ALLY a medical device? Is it regulated?

No. ALLY is a care-coordination tool that surfaces information based on established clinical guidelines and government schemes. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or deliver medical advice. Under HSA's 2025 SaMD/CDSS guidelines, this positioning sits explicitly outside the medical-device perimeter. Every AI output carries a clear disclaimer; emergency keywords route to 995.

Is my loved one's health information safe?

Yes. All data is stored in the Singapore region, encrypted at rest and in transit, and access-controlled to only the people you invite. ALLY is PDPA-compliant, has a designated Data Protection Officer, and follows MOH's Healthcare Sector Advisory Guidelines. We never sell your data and never train AI models on it.

How accurate is the discharge parser?

The parser is good and getting better, and we publish accuracy figures on our transparency page. Critically, every parsed care plan goes through a human-in-the-loop confirmation step before activation. You see the original document and the parsed output side by side. Nothing happens automatically that you didn't approve.

What if my helper doesn't speak English?

The FDW first-class mode supports Tagalog, Bahasa Indonesia, and Mandarin alongside English, with audio output and large-text layouts. Medication explanations, wound-care videos, and task notes are translated content — not just translated buttons. Available on the Family tier and above.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No phone calls, no friction. Cancel from the app and you keep access until the end of your billing period. We send a clear renewal reminder seven days before each charge.

Why is there a free tier?

Because some caregivers genuinely cannot afford to pay, and they are the ones who need the most help. The free tier covers the basics. The paid tiers fund the free tier. We hold this line on principle.

Will ALLY integrate with HealthHub or Singpass?

Singpass and MyInfo integration is on our Year 2 roadmap pending regulatory partnership. HealthHub integration is being explored through Synapxe's HealthX Innovation Sandbox. We'll announce these when they're ready, not before.

Can my hospital or employer partner with ALLY?

Yes — that's a core part of how ALLY scales. Email partners@ally.sg and we'll set up a conversation. We're actively looking for one orthopaedic ward, three employer pilots, and an AIC research partnership in the next twelve months.
